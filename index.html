How to use the workspace

App guide

A practical walkthrough of importing a loading guide, locating vans, updating yard positions, and sharing your records.

What this app does. Container Van Locator turns loading-guide records into a searchable yard list. Assign each van to its bay and block, see its stack position, and record moves and withdrawal status. The dedicated Actual arrivals page is paused. Use Add vans to enter containers already physically in the yard; use an additional loading guide to record new arrivals.

1. A complete yard workflow

Follow these steps when a new loading guide arrives or when you need to update van locations.

1Import the guideUpload an Excel/CSV file or select loading-guide photos on the Loading list page.
2Check the recordsReview photo extraction, van numbers, consignee names, and any LCL labels.
3Enter existing yard stockUse Add vans to record vans already placed and select each exact stack spot.
4Confirm in Yard viewOpen the bay and block to see each van in its exact stack slot.
5Keep the yard currentUse Quick shift when vans move, and update status after stripping or withdrawal.
6Back up or shareDownload a JSON backup or send a point-in-time snapshot link to a teammate.

2. Import loading guides and photos

From Loading list, use Browse files or drop a file into the upload area.

Spreadsheets and CSV

Excel, CSV, and TSV files are supported. The app looks for container/van number and consignee headings. If it cannot match them, choose the correct columns in the prompt. A CSV from Consignee Finder can be imported here.

Loading-guide photos

JPG, PNG, and WEBP photos can be selected together. The photo reader targets the printed Container No. and Consignee Name columns; it does not use the seal or shipper columns. Photos whose filenames contain a timestamp are read in timestamp order.

Review before adding

Check the extracted van number and consignee in the review table. Incomplete numbers and ISO container check-digit mismatches are highlighted and must be corrected before importing or exporting the photo CSV. Clear, straight-on photos give the reader the best chance.

Multiple consignees · LCL

When a van has two or more distinct consignee names, they are combined under that van and marked LCL. The Consignee Finder CSV export writes a separate row for each consignee, with the same van number and LCL type.

The photo CSV uses Container, Consignee, Type, Bay Location, Checked, with Vessel and Voyage appended. Handwritten yard notes on a photo are not used to assign the location; assign the bay and block in this app after import.

Adding a later guide

Import the additional guide from Loading list. New van numbers are added; matching numbers have consignee, vessel, voyage, and load type refreshed. Their existing yard location and status are kept. New rows wait for a location assignment.

Where incoming records appear

For now, use an updated loading guide to add incoming vans. The paused Actual arrivals page is not available for direct on-the-spot entry. New guide records appear in the Incoming full History filter.

3. Enter vans already in the yard

Use Add vans during setup to record containers that are physically in a bay before you begin regular operation.

  1. Enter the van number or identifier exactly as shown; Add vans accepts any text format. If it matches a Loading list record, its consignee and voyage details fill in automatically.
  2. For a new number, add the consignee and optional vessel and voyage. Multiple consignee names can be separated with semicolons and are marked LCL.
  3. Choose the bay/side and block, then tap the exact open tile in the stack diagram. Each tile shows the recalculated position number, stack, and level; occupied spots are disabled.
  4. Choose Add van to this location. The record and a time-stamped “Existing stock added” entry are saved in History.

The screen can be used repeatedly to build the opening stock list. You can later search any van, change its location, print a picking tag, or update its status.

4. Yard map, blocks, and stack numbering

Choose a bay in Yard view, then select one of its block tiles to see the vans and the visual stack.

Bay and block layout

Bay A has 11 blocks, Bay B has 12, Bay C has 7, Bay D has 5 front and 5 back blocks, and Bay E has 7 front and 7 back blocks. D/E labels show their side, for example D-F1 and E-B1. The whole yard has 54 sections/blocks and up to 594 van slots.

Four stack lanes

Each block has four lanes with capacities 3 / 3 / 3 / 2, for 11 vans maximum. The yard diagram labels FRONT and REAR. Use the displayed stack and level along with the position number.

Position #1 starts at the rear lane’s bottom. Number upward through that lane, then start at the bottom of the next lane toward the front. If a rear lane is empty, the first occupied slot in the next lane becomes #1. Numbers recalculate as vans move or leave the active yard.

Select an occupied tile to highlight its van. Select an open tile to assign a van to that exact stack slot. The inventory list also lets you change the stack position from its selector.

5. Search and assign a van

  1. Use the search box at the top and enter at least two characters from the van number or consignee name.
  2. Read the result for its van number, consignee, vessel/voyage, status, and current location.
  3. Choose Assign Bay / Block for an unassigned van, or Change location to move a placed van. Pick a bay/side and block, then confirm.
  4. Click a placed result to open its block in Yard view and highlight the exact slot. A withdrawn van opens its History records.

The Loading list also has bay/side and block selectors in each row. Assignments save automatically in the current browser.

6. Shift vans between blocks

Quick shift shows the source and destination block columns. Choose the source bay/side and block, then move a van to any available destination block—including another bay or the front/back section of D or E.

  • Scroll the block columns sideways to reach later blocks. The page keeps the working scroll position after a move.
  • On Android, swipe the columns horizontally; on a tablet, landscape view gives more room.
  • Each block holds up to 11 vans. Full blocks and occupied stack slots cannot be selected.
  • Every successful move updates the location and adds a time-stamped entry to History.

Open Yard Van Mover, the separate button-based app with its own name and forklift icon.

7. Status, dates, and History

In the Yard inventory row, use the status buttons to keep a van’s progress visible.

CY Stripped

Marks the van as CY Stripped and saves the date and time. It remains assigned to its yard position until moved or withdrawn.

Withdrawn

Marks the van withdrawn, records its time and last location, and removes it from the active yard layout. The record remains in the Loading list and History.

The History page shows incoming full records, withdrawals, location moves, and status changes, newest first. Use its filter or click a van’s history from Search to narrow the list. Times display in the device’s local time zone.

8. Print a picking tag

  1. Open the van’s block in Yard view.
  2. Find the van in the inventory list and choose Picking tag.
  3. Enter the FL operator name. This field is manually inputted.
  4. Choose Print picking tag and print or save it from the device’s print dialog.

The tag includes date, van number, consignee, FCL/LCL type, voyage, vessel, bay, block, stack and position, plus the FL operator.

9. Save, back up, share, and install

Saved on this device

The app automatically saves the list and History in this browser. It does not use an account or shared database, so another phone or computer will not automatically see your edits.

JSON backup

Choose Backup and keep the downloaded JSON file somewhere safe. Import that file later to restore or merge van records and History.

Share snapshot

Share snapshot creates a link with a copy of the current records and History. The recipient can view it and choose Save to this device. It is a point-in-time copy, not live syncing; later changes require a new link.

Android, laptop, and tablet

Open the HTTPS app link on Android in Chrome, then choose ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen. The same link works on laptop/desktop. Tablets can rotate to landscape. No separate APK is required.

A snapshot link contains the data visible in that snapshot. Share it only with people who should see those van, consignee, and location details. Photo OCR and Excel reading need an internet connection to load their free browser libraries; CSV import works without them.
